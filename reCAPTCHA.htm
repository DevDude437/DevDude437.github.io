Google changes how reCAPTCHA works

GO TO HOME PAGE

Google is changing how RECAPTCHA puzzles work. Now if the computer suspects that you aren't human, the standard picture solving puzzle will disappear and you will be forced to scan a QR code. The catch is, the scan must connect to Google servers in the background, which means for us degoogled Android users, this is a problem if you don't have Play Services installed on your device.
